The story

I know the feeling, because I lived it.

The chart looks perfect. You buy. It keeps climbing and for a moment you feel like you finally got one right. Then it stalls. You tell yourself it's just a dip. You watch it bleed and you don't sell, because selling would make it real. Then it's gone.

You close the app. You don't tell anyone. You lie awake doing the math on what that money could have been.

But the worst part isn't the money. It's the moment you look back and realize the signs were there the whole time. The wallets. The fake volume. The unlocked liquidity. Everything was screaming, and you couldn't hear it in time.

I went through that loop more times than I want to admit. Then I decided it would never happen to me again, and that it shouldn't have to happen to anyone else.

So I spent the last 7 months, day and night, building the tool I wish I'd had.

Antares

A free Chrome extension that sees the rug before you buy.

It runs automatically on the platforms you already trade on, and shows you what the chart won't: who really holds the supply, whether the volume is real, and whether there's an exit when it turns.
